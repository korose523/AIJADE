# Mac bulk-census decision: pre-execution interpretation

The separately published plan is unchanged. Its public introduction is
82cfaa2fd4ff7810cab09012829e5b8d7a2c82c9; its byte-identical first introduction
on the isolated research history is 3e37a85b5bb39669782543fc7197d73eecf2dc1f.
No observation, qualification or fresh construction is established by this note.

The plan's phrase "eight families" denotes the eight **integrity-fault** families
in the unchanged original registry. There are also the normal window and the
wrong-but-allowed-act semantic-limit window. The registry therefore retains ten
windows, ten pairs per block, four blocks, forty pairs and eighty condition rows.
No window, programme, denominator or placement is changed by this terminology.
The original seven/eight invalid-promotion/reuse opportunities per arm per block
remain twenty-eight/thirty-two over the confirmed comparison.

The C helper is a short-lived instrument process. Its returned PID/parent/start
record is retained. For an owned helper, RSS is the value obtained by Mach while
that exact helper was alive; parent-side Popen PID and the returned start identity
must agree. This exception cannot supply RSS for any other vanished process.
NoSuchProcess for any unrelated per-PID identity/RSS read is not an implicit zero.

Bulk KERN_PROC_ALL responses use the installed SDK's command field, including its
recorded length limit. This is sampled name-category evidence, not an invariant
full executable-name equivalence, an atomic census, or a proof of absence between
samples. Before/after responses, metadata and limit flags are retained. The exact
allowlisted GUI/server still have independently hashed full executable/argv
identity, listener and read-only empty-status evidence at every sample.

The sequence and block observers share an APFS capture lock derived solely from
the frozen helper's absolute path. A bounded nonblocking lock acquisition covers
native spawn through wait and raw parse, so two instrument children cannot enter
each other's live-RSS reads. The lock is opened without truncation or symlink
following and is checked for regular-file, owner and link-count identity. Lock
failure/timeout remains unknown. Lock evidence is retained; no admission is retried.

Ordinary owned workers retain the strict live identity/RSS rule. Before each tick,
the existing owned registry retires only independently observed absent/reused
identities. A worker that disappears during the actual capture/read is still
UNKNOWN, even after apparently normal work. Native self-RSS does not repair it.
This remaining lifecycle limitation is not a license to suppress a sample, pause
coverage at phase boundaries, or rerun a refused decision.

Apple documents this kernel interface as SPI. The native ABI and SDK are therefore
version-bound diagnostic evidence on this Mac; successful qualification would not
establish portability to another SDK, macOS version or machine.
