## Fixed checks and measurements

One fresh task process at a time, at most 15 CPU seconds, 30 wall seconds, 2 GiB sampled RSS and 64 MiB process output per task. Keep the sandbox's declared 2,000 ms and 512 MiB allocation settings. Record every task and case, errors and missing coverage, input and binary hashes and the actual frozen code commit. Do not merge or replace the earlier replay.

For every task measure UTF-8 lengths and hashes of generation and verifier A/B prompts produced by the actual C# builders. Replace the expected literals with a sentinel in a comparison-only task and require byte-identical prompts, including the full verifier input lists. This checks noninterference of expected outputs. Character or byte lengths alone do not establish token counts or context fit. Record tokenization as unmeasured unless a locally verified tokenizer is used.

Exercise the public-bank normal CLI with the reference-backed mock, seed 42, one trial, one round, twelve slots and pCorrect=1. Save the full simulation record. Check interrupted-prefix recovery and reject a changed bank identity or run fingerprint. These settings are engineering checks. They do not select tasks for the later model study or estimate model verifier errors. Add targeted regressions for loading, identity checking, allocation, prompt noninterference and resume compatibility.
